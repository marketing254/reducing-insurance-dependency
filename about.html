About RID Academy

Every dental practice in America should operate on its own terms - setting its own fees, choosing its own patients, and delivering care without insurance company interference.

Why We Exist

The Reality of PPO Contracts

Over 95% of dental practices struggle with PPO contracts that fail to keep pace with inflation and operational costs. The average practice loses 42-45% of revenue to PPO write-offs - that's $250,000+ per year for a typical single-doctor practice. Meanwhile, 31.8% of US dentists are actively planning to drop PPOs.
