# Addendum: putting a Member Network page on our other sites
**Written 30 Jul 2026. Read this together with LANDING PAGE - DEV HANDOFF.md.**

Lester wants a version of the Member Network page on each of our dental brand sites,
not just ThrivingDentist.com. This document says exactly what changes per site and
what must never change.

## The good news

`FOR WEBMASTER\index.html` is one self-contained file. Fonts, images, styles and
scripts are all inside it. So each site gets its own copy of that file with a handful
of edits. There is nothing to rebuild from scratch.

## What MUST change on every copy (4 things)

### 1. The nav bar
The file's nav currently shows the Thriving Dentist wordmark and their menu items.
Replace it with that site's own wordmark and its own real menu items, then add
"Member Network" as the LAST item, styled as a plain link identical to the others.
Never a button, never a different color. (Same rule as the Thriving Dentist version.)

### 2. The eyebrow line above the headline
Currently reads: FROM THE TEAM BEHIND THRIVING DENTIST
Change to: FROM THE TEAM BEHIND [that site's brand name]

### 3. The headline
**The Thriving Dentist headline CANNOT travel.** It is Gary Takacs' own signature
tagline ("Your practice should support your life. Not consume it.") and the lede
credits him by name ("You have heard Gary say it for years"). On a site where Gary
is not the face, that reads as borrowed or confusing. Each site needs its own
headline and its own lede. Lester approves these before build - see the table below.

### 4. The demo scenario (this is the one that makes each page feel native)
The hero demo shows a member problem and the written plan that comes back. Right now
it is a hygiene-production problem, which suits a general practice-management
audience. On an insurance-focused site, the visitor should see an INSURANCE problem
instead. Same layout, same "written reply, 2 to 3 business days" stamp, just a
problem that audience recognizes as their own. The three tab labels change with it.

## What must NEVER change (the honesty gates, all sites)

These are the same hard rules as the main handoff, and they apply to every copy:

- Hotline wording: "a written answer within 2 to 3 business days." Never "live,"
  never "24/7," never "AI chat."
- Launch wording: "Doors open the first week of August." Not "August 1."
- One price only: the $49 founding rate. The $99 and $199 member tiers live on
  dentalmembernetwork.com, not on these pages.
- No number on the library size. "A growing library," never "30+ kits."
- No founding-expert or free-for-life language. Those terms are private.
- The expert wall shows accepted people only, and the directory shows companies
  actually live at launch.
- Keep the "Illustrative example" label on the demo and the "real kits / real page
  from a member kit" chips. They are honesty features, not decoration.
- Every CTA still points to https://dentalmembernetwork.com.

## What NOT to do

Do not add links from these pages out to each other or to other brand sites in the
page body. Each page has exactly one job: move a warm reader to
dentalmembernetwork.com. Cross-links between sister sites belong in each site's own
footer, not in this page's content, because every extra outbound link is a chance to
lose the visitor before they click through.

## Order of work (recommended)

1. Ship the Thriving Dentist version first. It is finished and approved.
2. See what it does for traffic and signups for a week or two.
3. Then roll out to the next site, using what we learned.

Building six at once means six pages to fix if something in the copy needs to change.
One at a time means one fix.
