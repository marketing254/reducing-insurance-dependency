The short version: We only collect information you choose to share with us — such as when you fill out a form or register for a webinar. We do not sell your data. We keep things simple, secure, and transparent.

1. Who We Are

RID Academy (Reducing Insurance Dependence Academy) is operated by Ekwa Marketing, a digital marketing agency dedicated to helping dental practice owners build profitable, insurance-independent practices. This Privacy Policy applies to the website at ridacademy.com and all its pages and services.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at team@rid.academy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

We only collect information you voluntarily submit through our website forms. Depending on the form, this may include:

  • Waitlist & newsletter sign-up: your name and email address.
  • Webinar registration: your name and email when you request access to a replay or live session.
  • Partner / speaker application: professional background, contact details, and area of expertise.
  • Growth Engine consultation: practice details and contact information submitted through the consultation form.
  • Event registration: your name and email when you sign up for a summit or live event.

Information collected automatically

When you browse the site, we collect standard technical information such as pages visited, time on site, and general device and browser details. This data is anonymised and used only to improve the website experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share with us to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and fulfil any requests you have made.
  • Send you the webinar replay, resource, or event confirmation you registered for.
  • Send occasional updates about RID Academy content, events, and resources. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Review partner and speaker applications to facilitate collaborations.
  • Improve the site experience based on how visitors use it.
  • Meet any applicable legal obligations.

We will not use your information for any purpose beyond what is listed here without your prior consent.

4. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep the site running smoothly and to understand how it is being used.

Essential cookies

Required for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.

Analytics cookies

We use analytics tools to see which pages are most helpful and where we can improve. All data is anonymised — no individuals are identified.

Preference cookies

We store small browser cookies to remember choices you have made, such as whether you have dismissed a notification, so we do not show it again.

Managing your cookie preferences

You can adjust or clear cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling non-essential cookies will not affect your ability to use the site.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Full stop.

We work with a small number of trusted service providers to operate the website. Where relevant, your information may be shared with:

  • Google Workspace: form submissions are stored securely in Google Sheets for internal use only.
  • Zoom / Webinar platforms: when you register for a live session, your details are passed to the platform to facilitate the session.
  • Ekwa Marketing team: our internal team reviews form submissions to follow up on enquiries and applications.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Data Retention

We only keep your information for as long as it is needed:

  • Newsletter and waitlist sign-ups are held until you unsubscribe.
  • Webinar access requests are kept for 12 months after the event date.
  • Partner and consultation enquiries are held for up to 2 years.

Once information is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights over the personal data we hold about you, including:

  • Access: request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to correct any inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: request that we delete your data.
  • Objection: opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Portability: request your data in a transferable format.

To make a request, email team@rid.academy with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We take the security of your information seriously. The website is served over HTTPS and data stored through our services benefits from enterprise-level security. Only authorised team members have access to submitted information.

While we do everything reasonable to keep your data safe, no system is completely immune to risk. If you ever have concerns, please contact us directly.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website includes links to external platforms such as LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Spotify, and YouTube. This Privacy Policy covers RID Academy only. We are not responsible for how those platforms handle your data, and we encourage you to review their own privacy policies before engaging with them.

10. Children's Privacy

RID Academy is a professional resource for dental practice owners and industry professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted information through our site, please contact us at team@rid.academy and we will take prompt action.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our practices evolve or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
