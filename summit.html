Frequently Asked Questions

Is the summit really free? +

Yes, completely free. There are no hidden fees, no credit card required, and no premium tiers. We believe access to this education should be available to every dental practice ready to reduce their insurance dependence.

Will sessions be recorded? +

Yes, all registered attendees get on-demand access to all sessions. If you can't attend live, you'll be able to watch at your own pace for 30 days after the event concludes.

How do I earn CE credits? +

Attend the live sessions and complete a brief assessment to earn AGD-PACE approved continuing education credits. Credits are tracked and managed through our platform, and you'll receive your certificate immediately upon completion.

What technology do I need? +

Just a web browser and an internet connection. The summit runs entirely online through your browser - no special software to download or complex setup required. Works on desktop, tablet, or mobile.

Can my whole team attend? +

Absolutely! We encourage team participation. Register as many team members as you'd like - your front desk, hygienists, and office manager will all benefit from the content. It's a great opportunity to align your whole practice on the transition strategy.
